Before developing the HeySpiral app, I built and managed an online community focused on conversations around personal experiences, mental health awareness, relationships, identity, and personal growth.

This community became the foundation for understanding user behaviors, identifying recurring challenges, and discovering opportunities for a more intentional digital experience.

---

# Audience Development

During Summer 2025, my platform experienced significant growth through content centered around contemporary social topics, including:

- Mental health awareness
- Substance use conversations
- Relationships and dating
- Love and interpersonal connections
- Identity and self-reflection
- Personal growth

By creating content around topics that resonated with users, I gained experience understanding audience interests, engagement patterns, and the types of conversations that encouraged meaningful interaction.

---

# Audience Insights

Based on platform analytics and community interactions, I observed that the audience primarily consisted of young adult users, particularly individuals in their 20s.

The community included diverse users across genders and LGBTQ+ communities who engaged with discussions surrounding:

- Emotional well-being
- Personal struggles and life experiences
- Relationships and connection
- Self-reflection
- Mental health conversations

These interactions provided insight into the importance of creating digital spaces where users feel understood, supported, and connected.

---

# User Behavior Patterns

Through comments, engagement, and recurring community discussions, I identified several patterns:

- Users were seeking spaces for honest conversations and connection.
- Many users shared personal experiences and challenges.
- Some users expressed frustration with traditional social media environments.
- Users often wanted continued access to supportive communities even after leaving or deactivating social platforms.

These patterns helped identify an opportunity for a more intentional, user-centered platform experience.

---

# Product Opportunity

The insights gathered from community growth helped shape the vision behind HeySpiral.

The opportunity identified:

**How might we create a digital space where users can maintain connection, reflection, and access to supportive tools beyond traditional social media platforms?**

This question became the foundation for exploring HeySpiral as a product concept.
