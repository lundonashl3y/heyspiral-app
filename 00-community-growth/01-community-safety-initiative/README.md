As the HeySpiral community grew, user interactions revealed the importance of creating a safer and more supportive digital environment.

Through comments, messages, and community conversations, users shared experiences involving emotional distress, substance-related struggles, and moments where they expressed needing additional support.

These interactions highlighted the importance of incorporating trust and safety considerations into the product experience.

---

# Problem Identified

As community engagement increased, I identified a recurring need:

Users were looking for connection and support during difficult moments but did not always know where to find appropriate resources.

Key observations included:

- Users discussing personal challenges and emotional struggles.
- Community members seeking understanding and connection.
- Users expressing difficulty navigating support resources.
- The need for clearer pathways toward professional resources.

---

# 988 Awareness Initiative

To address this need, I developed a community awareness initiative focused on increasing visibility of the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

The purpose of this initiative was to:

- Increase awareness of available crisis resources.
- Encourage users experiencing crisis situations to seek professional support.
- Promote responsible conversations around mental health and well-being.
- Create a safer and more informed community environment.

The goal was not to replace professional crisis services, but to help connect users with existing support systems.

---

# Product Safety Considerations

This experience influenced how I think about trust and safety within HeySpiral.

Potential product considerations include:

- Making support resources easy to find.
- Creating clear pathways for users seeking help.
- Designing empathetic user experiences.
- Considering privacy and user safety.
- Building responsible community guidelines.

---

# Future Product Opportunity

Based on community feedback, I began exploring ways HeySpiral could provide users with continued access to supportive tools beyond traditional social media platforms.

Potential opportunities include:

- A dedicated resource center.
- Easy access to verified support information.
- Wellness-focused tools.
- User-controlled personal support features.
- Safer community interaction design.

---

# Product Management Skills Demonstrated

## Trust & Safety

- Recognized safety concerns through user feedback.
- Considered responsible product responses.
- Prioritized user well-being within product decisions.

## User Research

- Identified recurring themes from community interactions.
- Used qualitative feedback to understand user needs.
- Translated observations into product opportunities.

## Responsible Product Design

- Designed with empathy and user safety in mind.
