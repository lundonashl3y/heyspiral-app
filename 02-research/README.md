# Research & Product Discovery

## Overview

Research for HeySpiral focuses on understanding user behaviors, identifying unmet needs, and exploring opportunities for creating a more intentional digital experience.

Insights gathered from community interactions, audience engagement, and product exploration helped guide feature decisions and product direction.

---

# Research Goals

The primary goals of research were:

- Understand user motivations and behaviors.
- Identify challenges users experience on existing platforms.
- Discover opportunities for improving digital wellness experiences.
- Explore features that support connection, reflection, and personal growth.

---

# User Research Insights

Based on community interactions, several recurring themes emerged:

- Users value spaces where they feel understood.
- Many users seek meaningful conversations beyond traditional social media.
- Users are interested in tools that support personal growth and daily routines.
- Users benefit from accessible resources and supportive experiences.

---

# User Problems Identified

## Problem 1: Digital Burnout

Users may leave traditional social platforms due to overwhelming experiences but still want access to meaningful connection.

## Problem 2: Lack of Intentional Wellness Tools

Users may want simple tools to support reflection, habits, and personal goals.

## Problem 3: Difficulty Finding Support Resources

Users may struggle to locate helpful resources during challenging moments.

---

# Product Opportunities

Based on research findings, HeySpiral explores:

- Community-focused experiences.
- Wellness tracking tools.
- Reflection activities.
- Resource accessibility.
- Personalized user experiences.
