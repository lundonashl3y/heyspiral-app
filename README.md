HeySpiral is a community-focused product concept designed around creating a more intentional and supportive digital experience for users navigating conversations around mental health, relationships, identity, personal growth, and everyday challenges.

This repository documents my product development journey, including audience research, community insights, UX design, branding, product planning, and prototype development.

My goal with HeySpiral is to create a platform that users can carry with them beyond traditional social media experiences — providing a consistent space for reflection, connection, personal growth, and access to helpful resources.

---

# Product Vision

HeySpiral was created from observing a recurring pattern within online communities: users often seek connection, understanding, and support but may leave social platforms due to digital burnout, negative experiences, or choosing to deactivate their accounts.

The vision behind HeySpiral is to create a user-centered platform that allows individuals to maintain access to meaningful experiences, tools, and resources beyond a single social media platform.

The product explores how technology can support healthier digital interactions through community, self-reflection, and wellness-focused experiences.

# Community Growth & User Discovery

## Digital Content & Audience Development

During Summer 2025, I developed and grew a digital audience through content focused on contemporary social topics, including:

- Mental health awareness
- Substance use conversations
- Relationships and dating
- Love and interpersonal connections
- Identity and self-reflection
- Personal growth

Through audience engagement, comments, and community interactions, I gained insight into user interests, recurring conversations, and areas where users were seeking connection and understanding.

This experience became the foundation for identifying product opportunities and understanding the needs of the community HeySpiral aims to serve.

---

# User Insights & Product Opportunities

Through community interaction and audience research, I identified several recurring patterns:

- Users looking for spaces to discuss personal experiences and challenges.
- Users seeking meaningful connections beyond traditional social platforms.
- Users leaving or deactivating social media accounts while still wanting access to supportive communities.
- Opportunities to create more intentional and user-focused digital experiences.

These insights helped shape HeySpiral's product direction and feature exploration.

---

# Trust & Safety Considerations

Community feedback also highlighted the importance of responsible user support and safety-focused design.

Through interactions with users discussing serious personal challenges, I explored opportunities to create a safer digital environment by:

- Increasing awareness of available support resources.
- Encouraging users to seek professional support when needed.
- Creating empathetic user experiences.
- Considering trust and safety throughout product development.

A key initiative explored was increasing awareness of the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline as a resource for individuals experiencing crisis situations.

The goal is not to replace professional support services, but to help users better understand available resources and create safer pathways toward support.
